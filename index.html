How one cup is made

No premix, no powder. Every glass follows the same four steps.

  1. Grind the spices

    Cardamom, ginger and cloves are crushed fresh every morning.

  2. Boil the water and leaf

    Loose assam goes into water first, so the tea opens up before any milk is added.

  3. Add milk and sugar

    Full-cream milk joins the pot and is brought to a rolling boil twice.

  4. Strain and pour

    The chai is poured from a height into a steel glass or a clay cup.
