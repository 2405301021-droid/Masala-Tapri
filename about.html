About Masala Tapri

A neighbourhood tea shop built around one idea: chai should taste like someone made it for you.

Our story

Masala Tapri started as a single stove and a steel kettle outside a bus stop. Regulars asked for stronger tea, then for ginger, then for a place to sit. The shop grew from those requests.

Today we still make every pot by hand. We buy loose assam leaf from one supplier, grind our spices before opening and never use premixed powder.

What we stand for

Fresh spicesGround every morning in small batches, so the aroma is still in the cup.
Fair pricesNothing on the menu costs more than ₹100, because chai is an everyday habit.
Less wasteClay cups are biodegradable and we give a discount when you bring your own flask.

The team

Five people run the shop: two chai makers, one snack cook, one person on the counter and the owner, who still works the morning shift. If you come early, you will meet everyone.
